Our Story

How we met

It was a rainy October evening in San Francisco, and Sophia had ducked into a tiny bookstore to wait out the storm. Liam was already there, reaching for the very same poetry collection. They argued — politely — over who saw it first, and ended up sharing coffee instead of the book.

Six years, three cities, and one very spoiled golden retriever later, he proposed in that same bookstore, down on one knee between the poetry shelves. She said yes before he finished the question.
